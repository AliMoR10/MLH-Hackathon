# Code of Conduct

First PR Buddy exists to make open source welcoming for beginners, and we hold ourselves to the same standard.

**We expect everyone to:**
- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new
- Give feedback about code, not about people
- Respect different opinions, backgrounds and levels of experience
- Assume good intent and ask questions before judging

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks, or sharing someone's private information.

**Reporting:** if something goes wrong, contact a maintainer (see the Team table in the README) by opening a private message or a confidential issue. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that break this code.
